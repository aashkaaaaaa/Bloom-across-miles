💌 About

A bouquet for the ones far away

Bloom Across Miles lets you build a bouquet of hand-drawn pastel flowers, write a love letter, and send it to someone who is far from you.

How it works

  • Pick your flowers. Choose from ten flowers, then drag, rotate and resize each one. Add a wrapper and a ribbon.
  • Write your note. Add who it's for, who it's from, the distance between you, and your message.
  • Send it from your own Gmail. We open a Gmail draft addressed to them and copy a letter card for you to paste in. You press Send.
  • They open it. An envelope opens with confetti, your letter appears, and then the full bouquet, with what each flower means.

Free, with no account

There is nothing to sign up for. Your bouquet and letter travel inside the link itself, so there is no database holding them. The privacy page explains exactly what happens to your data.
