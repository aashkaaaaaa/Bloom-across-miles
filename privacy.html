🔒 Privacy

Privacy

The short version: no accounts, no database, no analytics, no cookies. Your bouquet and letter live inside the link you send.

Where your bouquet and letter are kept

When you press Send, your flowers, wrapper, ribbon, names, distance and message are packed into the bouquet link, in the part after the #. Browsers don't send that part of a link to the website's server, so the server hosting this site never receives your letter.

That also means anyone who has the link can open the bouquet. Share it only with the person it's for. The link is sent by email, so it is handled by your email provider and theirs like any other email.

Your unfinished bouquet

While you're building, your bouquet is saved in your own browser's storage on your device, so you don't lose it if you go to another page. It isn't sent anywhere. Pressing Send another, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes it.

Sending the email

This site sends in one of two ways, depending on how it's set up:

  • One-click sending: when you press Send, the recipient's email address, the "to" and "from" names, the distance and the bouquet link are passed to EmailJS, the email service that delivers the email from the site owner's Gmail account.
  • Gmail draft: the email address and letter fill in a Gmail draft that opens in your own Google account. They go from your browser straight to Gmail, not to us.

If you choose to copy the pink letter card, it is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it.

The distance calculator

If you use Work it out from two cities, each city name you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's place search (Nominatim) when you press Find, to look up where it is. Nothing else goes with it: not your bouquet, letter or email address. The cities you pick are remembered in your browser for next time and only the distance goes into the bouquet.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes (GitHub Docs). The fonts are served from this site. Apart from the optional distance calculator, pages don't load anything from other websites.
